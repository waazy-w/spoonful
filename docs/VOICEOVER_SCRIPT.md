# Voice-over script (record it in your own voice)

Two ways to record. Pick one.

- **A. Talk over the silent demo:** play `docs/spoonful-demo-silent.mp4` (2:28, no sound, no captions) and read the lines below. Start each line when its on-screen cue appears; the timestamps are a guide. Record with OBS, Loom, Clipchamp (built into Windows), or record your voice separately and drop it under the video in any editor.
- **B. Present the slides:** open the slide deck in Present mode and screen-record yourself. Each slide's speaker notes hold the same lines, and it takes about 2:30.

Tips: smile while you talk (it changes your voice), pause for a beat between scenes, and don't rush the "Same list, different day" part, because that's the heart of the pitch. A phone earbud mic in a quiet room is plenty.

| Time | When you see… | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Green title card, "Spoonful" | "Hi, I'm Saaz. Every to-do app assumes you have the same energy every day. For students living with chronic illness, ADHD or depression, that just isn't true. On a bad day, the list doesn't shrink. It just turns into guilt." |
| 0:19 | The app appears, with "Good morning" | "Spoonful is built on spoon theory. You get a limited number of spoons each day, and every task costs some. So it starts with one question: how much energy do you have right now?" |
| 0:33 | Example tasks load, "Great" is picked | "Here's a typical student week. On a great day, Spoonful plans the must-dos and deadlines first: the doctor's appointment, the email due today, even the big lab report. And it always keeps one spoon in reserve." |
| 0:50 | "Low" is picked; the plan shrinks | "Same list, rough day. Now only two things fit. Everything else is still here, with a reason: your energy is already spoken for, this can wait. No red badges. No shame." |
| 1:07 | "Break it down" is clicked | "The lab report is due tomorrow, but five spoons will never fit a low day. So we break it down, with built-in suggestions that work offline. Now, on an okay day, Spoonful plans just the first step. The deadline still moves forward." |
| 1:25 | Focus mode, one task in big text | "Focus mode shows one thing at a time. Done, not now, or too big. It can read each step aloud, and the gentle timer counts up, never down." |
| 1:38 | Settings: text gets bigger, then dark and high contrast | "Accessibility is built in: larger text, dyslexia-friendly spacing, high contrast, dark mode, and full keyboard support. Our automated accessibility check finds zero violations." |
| 1:54 | Black terminal with green test results | "Under the hood, the planner is a pure, tested engine: sixteen unit tests plus a browser accessibility test. No server, no account, and it works offline. Your health data stays on your device." |
| 2:10 | Green closing card with the web address | "Spoonful. Plan by energy, not by hours. Try it at waazy-w dot github dot io slash spoonful. Thanks for watching!" |

When you're done, upload your version to YouTube in place of the computer-voice one. The captions file `docs/spoonful-demo.srt` matches the narrated video, not your recording, so let YouTube auto-caption yours instead (then fix any mistakes in YouTube Studio → Subtitles).
